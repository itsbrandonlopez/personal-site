A little about me.

What I do and where I've been.

I work in live audio and AV systems — mixing shows, programming Q-SYS, and building control systems that actually make sense to the person running them. Hand me a rig that's been duct-taped together for five years or a problem nobody can find, and I'm genuinely happy. That's where I do my best work.

Most of my time goes to live events and freelance AVL/Q-SYS work — churches, corporate events, venues, integration companies across the country. I've show-called a 1,400-seat theatre, built volunteer teams from scratch, and been the one people call when a quick fix turns into something bigger. I show up, I don't panic, and I get things working.
